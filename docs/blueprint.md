# **App Name**: ChronicAI App

## Core Features:

- User Health Profile: Securely create and manage personal and clinical information, including chronic diseases and medication. Users can update their data as needed.
- Clinical Data Logging: Enable users to easily add new entries such as blood pressure measurements and new medications.
- Activity/Exercise Tracking: Allow users to record their physical activity and exercise sessions.
- AI Health Assistant: An interactive chatbot provides advice, recommendations, and therapeutic information tailored to the user's health profile and conditions. The AI will use reasoning as a tool to tailor information.
- Urgent Care Gateway: The AI health assistant is equipped with a tool to identify urgent health situations and provide clear instructions for contacting local health units.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A reassuring and professional blue (#3CC1DD), chosen to evoke trust and serenity suitable for health-related interactions. This will be used for key interactive elements and branding.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated blue (#F0F3F4) to ensure high readability and a calming visual foundation, reducing eye strain for older users.
- Accent color: A distinct, slightly deeper blue (#477EEA) to provide clear visual hierarchy and call attention to important actions and notifications, while maintaining a cohesive 'blue palette'.
- Highlight color: A warm and inviting yellow (#F7E06E) will be used sparingly for prominent calls-to-action or critical alerts, fulfilling the user's request for yellow in a harmonious and effective way.
- Main font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for all text. Its modern, clear letterforms combined with a touch of warmth ensure excellent legibility and approachability for the target age group, in both headlines and body text.
- Simple, recognizable, and moderately sized icons will be used throughout the interface. Solid or clearly outlined medical and general wellness-themed icons will enhance comprehension and ease of use without visual clutter.
- Spacious and clean layout with ample whitespace to prevent information overload. Key information will be presented clearly, often in card-like structures, with larger touch targets and a straightforward navigation flow suitable for seniors.
- Subtle and functional animations for transitions, data submission feedback, and chatbot response appearance. Animations will be smooth, purposeful, and avoid distractions to ensure a pleasant and predictable user experience.
